**Snapshot date:** July 2026  
**Purpose:** identify recurring capability signals, not claim a statistically
representative labor-market study.

## Extraction categories

For each JD, record:

- title, seniority, company, location and date accessed;
- education and experience eligibility;
- programming and SQL;
- statistics, ML/DL and GenAI;
- data engineering and databases;
- software engineering, testing and Git;
- cloud, containers, CI/CD and orchestration;
- deployment, monitoring and performance;
- security, privacy, responsible AI and governance;
- domain/product knowledge;
- communication, collaboration and leadership;
- required versus preferred.

## Frequency is not enough

Prioritize a skill using:

1. frequency across suitable roles;
2. whether it is required or preferred;
3. importance to actual job performance;
4. difficulty/time to acquire;
5. strength of current evidence;
6. relevance to target geography/company/domain.

## How contributors should update the map

1. Use official employer career pages where possible.
2. Sample at least five companies and 20 suitable postings for a market/role claim.
3. Preserve access date and avoid copying full copyrighted descriptions.
4. Summarize requirements; link to the source.
5. Separate entry, mid and senior roles.
6. Report sample limitations and expired links.
7. Never claim universal requirements from a small convenience sample.

## Recommended repository evolution

In a future data-backed release, store normalized, non-copyright-infringing metadata
and skill tags in a CSV/JSON file, then generate:

- role-by-skill frequency tables;
- region and seniority comparisons;
- quarterly change reports;
- links-last-checked status;
- roadmap gaps and update issues.

This should be a transparent maintained dataset, not scraped personal data or
republication of full job descriptions.
