# Curated Beginner Resource Library

## How selection works

Each topic has one **primary** resource. Alternatives serve a specific need: a more
visual explanation, extra practice, deeper reference, or structured certificate.
Do not complete every resource.

Legend:

- **Free:** core content is available without payment
- **Audit:** learning may be free; graded work/certificate can require payment
- **Paid:** payment required for the relevant content/exam
- **Reference:** consult as needed rather than read end to end

Prices and availability change. Verify on the provider page before enrolling.

## Paid-course decision rule

Pay only when it provides at least one of:

- assessed assignments with feedback;
- live mentoring or strong peer accountability;
- lab infrastructure you genuinely need;
- a credential requested in your target market;
- accessibility/language support unavailable in the free route.

Do not pay merely because the curriculum feels more official. Compare the syllabus
against this path and verify refund, renewal and regional pricing terms.
